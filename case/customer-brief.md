# Customer brief: Blue Ridge Delivery Co. (fictional)

Regional last-mile delivery. About 50 vehicles (vans, cutaways, box trucks), 50 drivers, 8 office staff, three depots. Roughly 120 miles per vehicle per day, 250 workdays.

## Stakeholders and what each cares about
| Role | Cares about | Pain they'd name |
|---|---|---|
| Operations Manager | Running the depot, on-time starts | "Drivers call all morning to confirm shifts." |
| Director of Finance | Predictable maintenance spend, payback | "Emergency repairs and tows are unbudgeted." |
| Safety & Compliance Coordinator | Audit readiness, inspection records | "When an inspector asks, files are slow to find or missing." |

## Current state
- Whiteboard schedule and paper binders for inspections and service
- No automatic service-interval tracking, so oil changes slip unnoticed
- Records scattered across depots

## What to fix first (agreed in discovery)
1. **Missed maintenance:** highest cost and a safety risk
2. **Audit-ready records:** comes almost free with #1, since every inspection is signed, filed, and searchable on submit
3. **Shift calls:** a daily drag, and the quickest win to show the team

## Why Workspace
The team already has phones. Calendar, Forms, Sheets, and Drive need no new hardware and little training, are priced per user, and can go live at one depot in days.

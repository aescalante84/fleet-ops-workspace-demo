# Talk track: "a driver's day"

Tie every screen to a pain point from discovery. Open each with the pain, then show the fix.

1. **Morning, shifts.** *Pain: drivers call to confirm.* Change one shift in Calendar; it appears on the driver's phone with the route, the van, and the inspection link. *"Managers spend the morning running the depot, not answering calls."*
2. **Pre-trip, inspection.** *Pain: problems found on the road.* The driver completes a one-minute Form with a photo. Flag a tire defect. The shop receives an alert in seconds, and the Sheet shows the van past its service interval. *"Caught in the yard, before it's a tow."*
3. **Audit.** *Pain: the binder scramble.* Open one van's folder: signed inspections and service history, searchable by unit number or VIN. *"Any van's file in under two minutes."*
4. **Value.** Show the model with the numbers they gave in the first nine minutes. State the base case and the range, and point out what is hard dollars versus capacity.
5. **Ask.** Propose the six-week pilot (see `pilot/pilot-plan.md`) and name the decision rule up front.

## Objection: "Isn't this just spreadsheets? Why not a telematics platform?"
Fair. Telematics earns its place when you want data straight from the engine. Driver-entered odometers are the weak point of this design, and I'd say so. Workspace stays your records and team layer either way, and it gets you value in days at a fraction of the cost. The pilot will show whether you need the next layer.

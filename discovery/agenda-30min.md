# 30-minute agenda: discovery inside the demo

| Minutes | Block | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0-9 | **Discovery:** their numbers and one shared problem | Get actuals for the value model and agree on a single problem to solve first |
| 9-13 | **Dispatch hub:** shifts on the driver's phone | Show the quickest win (shift calls) |
| 13-19 | **Digital inspection:** phone inspection, automatic service alert | Show the highest-cost win (missed maintenance): defect to shop alert in seconds |
| 19-30 | **Audit, value, and pilot:** vehicle file, value model, pilot plan | Land the number, then ask for the pilot |

## Discovery questions (first 9 minutes)
**Ops:** How many routine "am I working?" calls do you take a day, and how long does each take?
**Finance:** What did you spend on maintenance last year? How many roadside events, and what did the last tow cost you end to end?
**Safety:** How long would it take to pull the files for three random vehicles right now?
**All:** If we could fix only one thing in the first six weeks, which one?

## Rule of the meeting
Anything they tell you goes into the value model live. By minute 19 the slide should say their numbers, not mine.

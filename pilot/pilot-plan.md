# Pilot plan: 6 weeks, one depot

**Scope:** 10 vehicles and 12 drivers at the main depot. About $1,200 all-in.

| Week | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Agree the pilot | An owner for each measure, targets, and a start date |
| 1 | Baseline | Count shift calls, inspections, and file-pull time as they are today; set the dollar baseline from the last 12 months of invoices |
| 2-5 | Live | The depot runs on Workspace, with a weekly check-in |
| 6 | Readout | Before vs. after, then decide |

## Five success measures (agreed before the pilot starts)
1. Routine shift calls down 50% against baseline
2. 95%+ of inspections done on the phone
3. Zero missing inspection records
4. Same-day alerts for defects and service due
5. Any vehicle file produced in under 2 minutes

**Decision rule:** hit 4 of 5, and projected annual savings are at least 2x year-one cost, then roll out depot by depot.

## Financial measures at readout
- **Repair spend:** every repair tagged planned or emergency. Target: emergency repairs and tows a smaller share of spend than the prior 12 months.
- **Downtime:** unplanned van-down days times the customer's daily cost of a van down.
- **Labor:** dispatch hours lost to routine calls plus audit-pull hours, times loaded rate.

The readout replaces my model's benchmark inputs with the customer's invoices.
